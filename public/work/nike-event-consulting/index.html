Nike Event Consulting

Guest management, seeding and event project management

As a consultant for Nike, Sarah supported the planning and execution of events and brand activations, with a focus on guest management, seeding and overall event project management.

A project from Sarah Al-farhan’s portfolio, from her work as a consultant for Nike.

Client
Nike
Scope
Events and brand activations
Sarah’s role
Consultant: guest management, seeding, event project management

“The work combined strategy, project management, community building, and cultural engagement.”

Sarah Al-farhan

Context

An activation depends on who is in the room. The work focused on ensuring the right mix of creatives, athletes, industry voices and cultural figures were engaged with the brand, and on creating relevant touchpoints between Nike and its community.

Sarah’s role

Sarah developed and managed guest lists, coordinated invitations and attendance, and worked on product seeding and relationship management. She also supported the wider event process: stakeholders, timelines, logistics and on-the-ground execution.

Execution

  • Developing and managing guest lists
  • Coordinating invitations and attendance
  • Product seeding and relationship management
  • Coordinating stakeholders, timelines and logistics
  • On-the-ground execution on the day
